Equipment Inventory

Privacy policy

Effective 1 November 2026

Equipment Inventory is a work tool for keeping track of equipment. It holds the details of the people who use it, a record of what they do in it, and information about the equipment itself. We use that information to run the system and keep it secure. We do not sell it, and the system does not use advertising or tracking.

1. Who is responsible

2. What we collect and why

InformationWhat it includesWhy we use it
Your account First name, last name, email address, your role (Administrator or Editor), whether the account is active or suspended, whether your password is temporary, and how many sign-in attempts have failed in a row. Your password is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form, so nobody can read it. To create your account, sign you in and control what you are allowed to do.
Activity records The date and time, the kind of event (such as signing in, a failed sign-in, signing out, a password change, or an account or equipment change), who did it, which account or item it affected, the IP address, and a short note. To keep the system secure, to find misuse, and to show who changed what.
Equipment records Serial numbers, manufacturers and models, manufactured month, the property, location and region where each item is, and which Editor looks after each property. To run the inventory and produce reports.
Photos of equipment Image files that people upload for an item, with the file name. To help recognise the equipment. Please do not upload photos that show people.
Reports and downloads Lists of equipment that you generate and, if you choose, download as a CSV file. To let you analyse the inventory. Reports show only what you are allowed to see.
Emails we send you When an Administrator creates or reactivates your account, an email with a temporary password. To let you sign in for the first time. You are asked to choose your own password straight away.
Technical records Server logs that can include your email address and what went wrong when an error occurs. To keep the system running and fix problems.

3. Cookies and the mobile app

The website uses only the cookies it needs to work:

  • A session cookie that keeps you signed in. You are signed out after 10 minutes of inactivity.
  • A security token inside forms, which protects them from being sent by another website on your behalf.

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and we do not load anything from other companies’ websites on these pages.

The mobile app keeps your password in memory only while the app is open and asks for it again when you sign in next time. It uses the camera or photo library only when you choose to add a picture to an item.

4. Who can see your information

  • Administrators can see every account, the activity records and all equipment.
  • Editors can see and manage the equipment at the properties assigned to them, and their own account.
  • Other people who use the system see only your name where it appears with a property or an equipment report.
  • We do not sell personal information. It is shared only with the services that run the system for us, such as hosting and email, and only so they can provide that service. The data is hosted in Kingston, Jamaica.
  • We will disclose information if the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

  • Accounts are kept until an Administrator deletes them.
  • Activity records are kept for at least 365 days and then deleted automatically. Because of this, a record can still mention your email address for that long after your account has been deleted.
  • Equipment records and photos are kept until an Administrator or the responsible Editor deletes the item. A photo is deleted with its item.
  • Technical records are kept only as long as needed to keep the system secure and working.

6. How we protect it

  • Passwords are stored in hashed form, and temporary passwords must be changed at first sign-in.
  • What you can see and do depends on your role. Editors cannot reach equipment at other people’s properties.
  • An account is suspended after 5 failed sign-in attempts in a row.
  • You are signed out after 10 minutes of inactivity.
  • The system is meant to be used over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.
  • Every sign-in and every change to accounts and equipment is recorded.

No system is perfectly secure. If something happens that puts your information at risk, we will tell you as the law requires.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct information that is wrong. An Administrator can change your name and email address;
  • delete your account, subject to the activity records described in section 5;
  • stop using your information in ways you object to, where the law allows.

Write to privacy@example.org. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the data protection authority in your country.

This system is for staff and other authorised users. It is not meant for anyone under 18.

8. Changes and contact

If we change how we use personal information, we will update this page and its effective date. Questions about this policy go to privacy@example.org.
